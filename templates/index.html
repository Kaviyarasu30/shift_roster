Shift Roster Generator

1. Team Information

Pick any range — 2 weeks, 3 weeks, a full month, or a range that crosses into the next month (e.g. 9 Jun to 9 Jul). The roster covers every day from "From date" to "To date", inclusive.

2. Employees & this month's shift

Each employee stays on ONE shift for the whole month. Pick where they go this month — e.g. if someone was on Morning last month and should move to Night, just select Night for them below.

EmployeeShift this month

"Rotate forward" moves every dropdown Morning → Evening, Evening → Night, Night → Morning — a quick starting point if your whole team rotates together each month. You can still adjust anyone by hand afterwards.
